DRAFT PLAN

Kelowna → Banff & Jasper

A December road trip for four, and a whole lot of frozen waterfalls.

4 travelers
Target: Dec 17–22
Route Options

Two ways to do it

Both start and end in Kelowna. Option 1 adds Jasper and the Icefields Parkway; Option 2 keeps it simple with more time in one place.

Option 1

Kelowna → Jasper → Banff → Kelowna

Kelowna → Jasper652 km · ~7.5–8.5 hrs
Jasper → Banff (Icefields Pkwy)288 km · ~5 hrs
Banff → Kelowna480 km · ~6.5–7 hrs
Total driving~1,420 km
6 days / 5 nights — 2 in Jasper, 3 in Banff. Sees both parks, but two long winter driving days.
Option 2

Kelowna → Banff only

Kelowna → Banff480 km · ~6.5–7 hrs
Banff → Kelowna480 km · ~6.5–7 hrs
Total driving~960 km
5 days / 4 nights, all in Banff/Lake Louise. More relaxed, cheaper, less winter-driving risk.
Timing

Suggested dates

Peak pricing hits hard from Dec 20 – Jan 2 (Christmas / New Year's). The window right after Dec 15 is the sweet spot before that surge.

Suggested: Thu Dec 17 – Tue Dec 22, 2026 — adjust once everyone confirms availability.

Book Airbnbs and any guided icewalk tours (Johnston Canyon / Maligne Canyon) as soon as dates lock — both sell out and rise in price closer to the date.
Good to Know

Winter driving reality check

  • Snow tires or chains are mandatory on the Icefields Parkway Nov 1 – Apr 1
  • No gas stations on the Icefields Parkway in winter — fill up fully first
  • Parkway maintenance runs 7am–3:30pm — plan to be off it before dark
  • Moraine Lake Road is closed to vehicles Oct–June (avalanche risk) on both options

Highlights

Must-see spots

Banff area
Banff
Banff Gondola / Nightrise

Ride up Sulphur Mountain; evening light-and-sound winter experience at the top.

Banff
Banff Upper Hot Springs

Outdoor mineral hot springs with mountain views — great after a cold day outside. ~$19.75/adult.

Banff
Johnston Canyon Icewalk

Frozen waterfalls and ice formations along a canyon trail, ~25 km from Banff. Guided tours include cleats/poles/transport.

Banff
Banff Christmas Market

Held at the historic train station — local artisans, hot cocoa, holiday atmosphere.

Banff
Lake Louise

Frozen lake, ice skating rink, Chateau Lake Louise. Moraine Lake Rd is closed to cars in winter.

Banff
Rotary Festival of Trees

Community holiday event with decorated trees, wreaths, gingerbread houses.

Jasper area (Option 1 only)
Jasper
Maligne Canyon Ice Walk

Guided walk into a frozen canyon among ice sculptures and mid-flow frozen waterfalls.

Jasper
Pyramid / Mildred Lake skating

Some of the best-maintained outdoor rinks in the Rockies, with mountain backdrops.

Jasper
Athabasca & Sunwapta Falls

Partially frozen waterfalls, easy short stops along the Icefields Parkway.

Jasper
Marmot Basin

Local ski hill if anyone wants a ski/snowboard day.
