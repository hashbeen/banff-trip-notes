Kelowna → Banff Road Trip

December 2026 · planning notes for the four of us

📅 Targeting Dec 15–21
🧍 4 people, 1 vehicle
💰 Target ~$900/person
🚗 From Kelowna, BC

Route: pick one

Leaning this way

Option 2 — Kelowna → Banff direct

  • ~830 km each way via Hwy 1 (Rogers Pass)
  • 5–7 days, base out of Banff/Canmore
  • More time actually at destinations, less driving fatigue
  • Banff/Lake Louise stay fully open & active in winter
Bigger trip

Option 1 — Kelowna → Jasper → Banff loop

  • ~610 km to Jasper, then ~290 km Icefields Pkwy to Banff
  • 7–9 days, more ground covered
  • Some Jasper attractions closed for winter (SkyTram, Glacier Centre, Miette Hot Springs)
  • Adds a second mountain pass with avalanche-closure risk

Must-see spots

  • Lake Louise — frozen lake, skating rink, snowshoe trails
  • Moraine Lake Road — usually closed to cars in winter, check status
  • Johnston Canyon Icewalk — frozen waterfalls
  • Banff Gondola — Sulphur Mountain views above the snow line
  • Banff Upper Hot Springs — outdoor hot springs, mountain views
  • Downtown Banff Ave — dinner, shops, holiday lights
  • Bow Falls / Surprise Corner / Vermilion Lakes — easy scenic stops
  • Canmore — quieter/cheaper base, Grassi Lakes trail

Before the trip

  • Confirm winter tires on the vehicle (mandatory on BC mountain highways Nov 1–Apr 1)
  • Book Airbnb (Dec fills up faster than expected)
  • Book hot springs / ice walk tickets in advance
  • Buy Parks Canada pass online ahead of time
  • Pack an emergency kit — blanket, snacks, water, phone charger, shovel
  • Check attraction winter hours before locking the itinerary

During the trip

  • Check DriveBC / 511 Alberta each driving morning (Rogers Pass avalanche control)
  • Build in one flex day with no driving in case of weather
  • Keep the gas tank above half through the mountain sections
